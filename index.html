Our Heritage

Founded in 1968, Salkia Shri Vikram Balika Vidyalaya has stood as a beacon of female education in Howrah for over five decades. As a Government Aided Higher Secondary School, we are deeply committed to providing quality education rooted in strong cultural values.

Our distinctive coral and pink campus has nurtured generations of young women, giving them the knowledge, confidence, and discipline to succeed in life. We believe in holistic development, where academic rigor meets character building.
