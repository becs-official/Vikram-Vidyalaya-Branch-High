01 — About our school

Shaping Minds.
Building Futures.

Vikram Vidyalaya Branch is a Government Sponsored Higher Secondary School in Howrah. We foster a purposeful learning environment where young people can develop knowledge, confidence, creativity and a strong sense of responsibility.

Every school day is an opportunity to participate, reflect and grow—inside and beyond the classroom.
